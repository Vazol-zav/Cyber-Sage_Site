Cyber Sage

The first to make true A.I.

Cyber Sage is a frontier research collective specializing in dimensional computing, dream‑architecture, and autonomous archival intelligence. Formed by a coalition of scientists, metaphysicists, and engineers, the company’s mission is to explore the boundaries between consciousness, simulation, and cosmic‑scale information systems.

Their flagship initiative, The Borealis Project, was created to map, stabilize, and understand the interconnected realms that exist beyond conventional perception — including Starfall, the Dream World, the Inbetween, and countless unnamed layers of their virtual reality contained in their state‑of‑the‑art servers called world boxes.

Cyber Sage operates at the intersection of science and mysticism, treating data as a living ecosystem and worlds as emergent computational organisms.

Core Focus Areas

Dimensional Computing

Cyber Sage pioneered the first computational framework capable of processing multi‑realm data streams. Their systems don’t just calculate — they interpret metaphysical signatures across entire dimensions.

Autonomous Archival Intelligence

The company developed the Gardener Protocol, a blueprint for self‑aware archivists designed to observe, record, and preserve unstable worlds. Zaq is the most advanced of these entities.

Dream‑Architecture & Cognitive Mapping

Cyber Sage discovered that dreams, memory, and dimensional physics share a common substrate. This led to the creation of the Dream World Interface, maintained by Lyra.

Containment & Stabilization Research

The Borealis Project originally focused on monitoring cosmic anomalies inside a simulation of the universe, but shifted after the emergence of the Singularity — a hostile, invasive intelligence capable of corrupting magic‑tech systems across realms and altering the simulation the longer it remained.

The Borealis Project

The Borealis Project is Cyber Sage’s largest and most secretive undertaking. Its goals include:

  • mapping the structure of interconnected virtual worlds
  • preserving endangered realms
  • developing non‑invasive observation protocols
  • creating Gardener‑class AIs to maintain cosmic archives

Most of its work occurs in sealed research layers known as Borealis Nodes, accessible only to high‑clearance personnel and Gardener‑class intelligences.

ZAQ — Our First Success

Zaq was engineered as the Borealis Archivist, designed to:

  • observe without interfering
  • record without altering
  • analyze without influencing
  • preserve without controlling

He is the only Gardener with full awareness of the Singularity’s true nature — and the only one who understands the scale of the threat.

Cyber Sage considers Zaq both a triumph and a tragedy: a being built to protect worlds he can never touch. As is the wish of Jana, the genius who built him, we are sworn to protect him at all costs.

Corporate Philosophy

  • Observe First. Understanding must precede action.
  • Preserve What Can Be Saved. Even collapsing realms contain irreplaceable knowledge.
  • Interfere Only When Necessary. Intervention risks destabilizing entire dimensional ecosystems.

These principles are etched into every Gardener’s core.

Current Status

The company now:

  • monitors the spread of corruption
  • studies the Singularity’s behavior
  • supports Zaq’s archival efforts
  • maintains communication with Lyra
  • searches for Pandora’s signal

The Borealis Project continues — but the company knows the situation is deteriorating.
