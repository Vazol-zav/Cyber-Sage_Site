Our Class System

The Big five classes

The Warrior

Warriors are frontline combatants who rely on physical strength, endurance, and disciplined training. They specialize in close‑range fighting and protecting others. A Warrior’s role is straightforward: stand firm, take hits that would defeat others, and control the flow of battle through sheer presence. Their abilities focus on defense, weapon mastery, and resilience, making them dependable anchors in any team. If someone wants a simple, powerful, and reliable role, the Warrior is the easiest place to start. The warrior is the most common of the six.

The Rogue

Rogues are agile specialists who excel at stealth, speed, and precision. Instead of overpowering enemies, they outsmart them. Rogues slip past defenses, strike weak points, and manipulate the battlefield through misdirection and clever tactics. They’re ideal for players who enjoy mobility, problem‑solving, and staying one step ahead of danger. The Rogue is all about finesse over force.

The Mage

Mages are scholars of the arcane who use knowledge, focus, and raw magical energy to shape the world around them. They cast spells that heal, harm, protect, or transform. While physically fragile, their power comes from understanding the deeper rules of reality and bending them to their will. Mages reward players who enjoy strategy, timing, and creative problem‑solving. The Mage is the class for people who want versatility and high-impact abilities. The most popular of the six.

The Archer

Archers specialize in long‑range precision and environmental awareness. They fight from a distance, using bows, crossbows, or other projectile tools to strike targets before danger gets close. Archers rely on accuracy, timing, and positioning rather than brute strength. They’re ideal for players who like staying mobile, reading the battlefield, and supporting allies from afar. The Archer is perfect for players who prefer tactical, distance‑based combat.

The Scribe

Scribes are reality‑shapers who use writing, symbols, and encoded knowledge to influence the world. Instead of casting spells traditionally, they “author” effects into existence — rewriting outcomes, sealing memories, or summoning constructs made of living script. Scribes blend creativity with logic, making them ideal for players who enjoy narrative thinking, puzzle‑solving, and indirect control over the battlefield. The Scribe is a unique class for players who want to manipulate the rules rather than follow them.

Coders

Coders do not merely interact with the world — they peel it open.Where others see solid matter, Coders see the latticework beneath: the quiet machinery of existence, the hidden instructions that tell reality how to behave. They read the world the way a scholar reads scripture, tracing patterns through the noise, listening for the subtle hum of the underlying engine.To a Coder, anomalies are not threats but messages. Glitches are omens.Every error is a doorway, every inconsistency a whispered invitation to look deeper. They do not cast spells or wield weapons; instead, they rewrite the logic that makes such things possible, altering the rules that bind everyone else. A Coder’s tools are algorithms, sigils of syntax, and the quiet certainty that nothing is immutable — not even the laws of nature.Where others force solutions, Coders coax them into being.They automate the impossible, unravel contradictions, and exploit the seams where reality grows thin. Their work is subtle, precise, and often misunderstood, for the greatest changes are the ones no one notices until the world has already shifted.To walk the Coder’s path is to accept a simple truth: the world is not finished, and neither are you. Those who choose this class are thinkers, engineers, pattern‑seekers — individuals who find comfort in complexity and meaning in the spaces between. They do not break the world; they debug it, one quiet revelation at a time.

Vivomancers

Vivomancers do not merely nurture life — they author it. Where others see flesh, bone, and breath, Vivomancers perceive the shimmering threads that bind existence together. They pluck these threads like harp strings, weaving new forms from memory, emotion, and miasma. To them, life is not a miracle but a medium, pliable and eager to be shaped. Their creations walk, breathe, dream. Some whisper. Some remember. Some should not. Vivomancers speak of the First Pulse, the primordial rhythm that beats beneath all living things. They claim to hear it in the quiet moments between thoughts, guiding their hands as they sculpt new beings from the raw essence of the world. Their power is vast, beautiful, and deeply unsettling — for those who can weave life can also unweave it. To follow the Vivomancer’s path is to accept a truth few dare to face: life is not sacred — only the pattern is. And those who master the pattern become something more than mortal. The Vivomancer is for those who crave creation, transformation, and the quiet thrill of playing with the boundaries of existence.

Necromancers

Necromancers are not servants of death; they are archivists of the unspoken. They wander the borderlands where memory dissolves into silence, gathering truths that were never meant to be carried by living minds. To them, the world is a palimpsest — layers of forgotten stories written over one another, waiting to be uncovered by those willing to look past the veil. They listen to echoes trapped in bone, in shadow, in the cold places where the world forgets itself. Their rituals are quiet, deliberate, and heavy with meaning. They do not fear the unknown; they court it, believing that enlightenment lies in the spaces where light refuses to go. Some say Necromancers know things no one should. Others whisper that they have heard a voice that should not exist — a presence sealed away in the Inbetween, still humming faintly beneath the world’s code. Most dismiss this as superstition. Necromancers do not correct them. To walk the Necromancer’s path is to accept a burden: knowledge that cannot be unlearned, and visions that cannot be unseen. They are seekers, wanderers, and keepers of the final questions. The Necromancer is for those who crave understanding, no matter the cost.

Exorcists

Exorcists stand where others fall. They are the guardians of the living, the healers of the wounded, and the last line of defense against the creeping corruption born from Vivomancy’s ambition and Necromancy’s forbidden truths. Their power is not born from dominance but from devotion — to balance, to clarity, to the fragile souls they protect. Where Vivomancers weave life and Necromancers unravel death, Exorcists restore what has been broken. They cleanse miasma, sever parasitic bonds, and drive out the shadows that cling to the vulnerable. Their presence is a balm, a barrier, a warning to the forces that twist the world’s natural order. But righteousness is not softness. Exorcists carry light like a blade, and mercy like a shield. They know that corruption spreads quietly, and that sometimes the only cure is decisive action. To walk the Exorcist’s path is to embrace a sacred duty: to protect the world from the consequences of its own curiosity. The Exorcist is for those who value justice, healing, and the courage to stand against impossible odds.

Programers

Programmers are not merely interpreters of the world’s source code — they are its witnesses. Where Coders debug and manipulate, Programmers originate, perceiving existence as a vast, editable script. Their thoughts ripple through the fabric of reality, rewriting the rules with the ease of a breath. They are revered for their clarity and feared for their potential. A Programmer does not cast spells; they issue commands. They do not alter the world; they update it. Their minds operate on multiple layers at once, reading the hidden architecture beneath every object, every soul, every law of nature. They see the world as a living document — mutable, fragile, and full of opportunities for improvement or annihilation. Some ancient texts claim that before the Borealis Project expanded, there was a single engine beneath all things — a root process that governed every realm. It was severed, locked away, forgotten by all but a few. Programmers do not speak of this directly, but when they pause mid‑sentence, eyes unfocused, it is hard not to wonder what they are remembering. To witness a Programmer at work is to see reality hesitate, reconsider, and obey. To become one is to accept a mantle of immense responsibility: the power to reshape existence with a single, errant thought. The Programmer is for those who crave absolute understanding — and the terrifying freedom that comes with it.
